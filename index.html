Shortcuts & Tips
Keyboard
⌘+K
Quick Capture — open the brain-dump popover from anywhere. Type up to 80 chars, hit Enter to save. Press again or Esc to close.
⌘+scroll
Calendar zoom — hover the calendar, hold Cmd/Ctrl, scroll up to zoom in (more chips, taller cells), down to zoom out. Pinch on trackpad works too.
⌘+=
Calendar zoom in — 10% step.
⌘+−
Calendar zoom out — 10% step.
⌘+0
Calendar reset zoom — back to 100%.
Enter
In the Add Task modal, submits and closes. In Quick Capture, saves the dump.
Esc
Closes any open modal or popover.
Mouse & drag
⇄
Drag a task from the Bank onto a calendar day to schedule it. Drag back to the bank to unschedule.
⇕
Drag the handle below the all-day strip to make it taller or shorter. The cap auto-derives from height.
⇕
Drag the slim handle between the calendar grid and the day card in the Todo List to resize the split.
⇄
Drag the vertical divider between Bank / Today / Calendar columns to adjust column widths.
2×
Double-click any calendar day to open a Google-Calendar-style quick-add popover for that date.
›
Click "May 2026" in the Add Task date pickers to pick any month + year.
✏️
Click the emoji next to "Todo List" to change the page icon — 30-emoji palette matched to the aesthetic.
Cognitive scaffolds
▸
First Step — when adding a task, fill in "the 5-second move" (e.g. put left shoe on). It shows as a soft blue bar under the task and is what your brain sees first — friction-free entry instead of the full chore.
🧠
Quick Capture — press ⌘K from anywhere to dump a 3-word idea. Review them later via the 📥 Inbox button on the Today card. Each capture can become a task with one click.
🔒
Dopamine Gate — tag tasks as Execute (cortisol) or Plan (dopamine). Plan tasks dim until you log 15 min of execute work today. Soft enforcement: dimmed, not blocked. Default 15 min, configurable.
●
Domain Modes — tag tasks with a domain (school, lifting, drums…). Each domain runs in Active or KTLO (keep-the-lights-on) mode. KTLO tasks fade so they don't compete with active builds. Toggle in the Cognitive scaffolds section.
⏱
Time Anchor — when enabled, fires a discreet toast every 25 min during focus sessions: "Time check: 3:47 PM · check on your people." Externalizes the internal clock that hyperfocus suppresses.
Hidden details
  • The all-day strip caps each column at a height-derived number of items — drag the handle to change density.
  • The calendar grid fits one viewport without scrolling at zoom ≤ 100%. Zoom in to expand hour rows for finer time precision.
  • Tasks with subtasks show a ▸ 0/N steps chip; click to expand inline. Each step can have its own scheduled date via the 📅 icon.
  • Hover a row in the Bank to reveal the ✕ delete (left-red on hover) and the ⧉ ✎ action buttons.
  • Bank rows auto-color their left edge by urgency: red (overdue) → orange (24h) → yellow (week) → blue (later).
  • Toasts stack at most 3 — older ones evict to keep the screen calm.
  • The Archive on the Todo List shows today's completions only — yesterday's stay in storage but don't pollute today's view.
  • Press the Today button on the calendar to jump back to the current week / day.
  • iCal imports preserve recurring events (RRULE), exceptions (EXDATE), time zones, and per-calendar colors.
Turn things off

Every scaffold is opt-out. Open Settings (⚙ in the sidebar) → Scaffolds to toggle First Step, Quick Capture, Dopamine Gate, Domain Modes, or Time Anchor individually. The app falls back to a clean classic todo list when all five are off.

Welcome to Focus Hub

A quick tour of the four main areas of the app. You can reopen this anytime from the ? button in the sidebar.

◈
Dashboard
Your daily snapshot — today's progress, habit streaks, focus minutes, and what to work on next. Quick add at the top lets you type tasks in plain English.
≡
Todo List
Three columns: Task bank (left), selected day (middle), calendar (right). Double-click any calendar day to quickly add a task for that date. Drag tasks from the bank onto a date to schedule.
□
Calendar
Full-month view. Add events (exams, classes, deadlines) or import an .ics file. Double-click a day to add a task for it. Color-coded by type.
◎
Focus
Pomodoro timer with auto-personalization. Picks 25/5, 50/10, or 52/17 — or learns your ideal focus length over time. Reports show distraction patterns.
Pro tips: Press Enter after typing in Quick Add. Drag tasks from the bank onto Habits to convert them. Use the S/M/L in the sidebar to change density.
